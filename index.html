The problem

Most research antibodies can't be trusted.

50%+
Over half of research antibodies fail validation in at least one application. Irreproducible reagents waste budgets and retract conclusions. Our answer: affordable, recombinant, application-validated antibodies with knockout controls. Source: Ayoubi et al., YCharOS, eLife 2023.
